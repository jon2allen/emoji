Miso was not supposed to live in Building B . He was a 🐟 stray tabby who slipped through the laundry room door on the 3rd floor and decided the apartment complex was his kingdom . Every morning he woke up on Mrs. Lee’s radiator , stretched , and watched the hallway lights flicker on/off .

By 7am the 🐰 building came alive . Kids raced to school , the elevator dinged , and the smell of coffee and garlic drifted from the kitchen vents. Miso patrolled the mailboxes , knocked over a stack of flyers , and left tiny paw prints on the polished floor. The security guard just smiled and left out tuna for him. 🚤

The 5th floor was the best. There was a balcony jungle 🦊 where Mr. Chen grew basil and a sunny windowsill where Miso could nap for hours 2️⃣ . One rainy Tuesday , he got locked in the stairwell and meowed until little Aisha from 4B heard him. She opened the door with a squeaky key and gave him a cardboard box . He claimed it forever.

Miso became the building’s unofficial mayor . He attended rooftop sunsets , judged the pet parade , and mediated disputes between pigeons and the super’s cat . When the power went out , he led everyone to the emergency stairs by glowing eyes and a brave little meow .

Now every resident knows the routine. If you hear soft thumps on the ceiling , see a tail flash behind a door , or find a warm lump on your shoes , it’s just Miso doing his rounds. He may not pay rent , but he pays in purrs and the building feels more like home .
